Privacy policy

Effective 1 October 2026. Operator: Open Horizon Labs. Contact: pit@urch.eu.

What we collect: account email and identity, exact text you choose to save, its content hash and first receipt time. We use these to authenticate you, preserve text, deduplicate it and return the passages you request. We do not classify text or require its source.

Supabase provides authentication and database storage. Cloudflare hosts the website and MCP endpoint. When you connect ChatGPT or Codex, requested excerpts are returned to that application under its own data practices. These service providers process data to operate the service; hosting may involve international transfers.

We retain saved text until you erase it or request erasure. Erasure removes it from the live SaaS database; provider backups can retain historical copies until their configured retention expires. Downloads and copies already returned to connected applications are outside our control. Account identity remains in our shared authentication service until separately resolved through support.

There is no application logging of saved text, search queries or access tokens. Cloudflare and Supabase may retain security and operational records under their provider policies. Temporary per-user request counters protect service availability. We do not sell your text or use it for advertising.

Controls: export from the signed-in account page, erase saved text there, revoke connected applications through your client, or contact pit@urch.eu for access, correction, deletion and other privacy requests. We verify account ownership. Only share text you are authorized to send; do not store authentication secrets, payment card details, protected health information or government identifiers.
